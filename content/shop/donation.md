Part of what we love about skating and this community is the low barrier of entry. If you can afford even the cheapest pair of skates, you can join. We want to keep running the event and keep it free for all, but as we've grown, so have our expenses.

If you are able, please consider donating to the Empire Skate Club so that we can continue hosting and growing the BAR.
